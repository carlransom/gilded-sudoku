How to play

1 · Tap a cell — pick an empty square on the board.

2 · Tap a number — fill it in. Every row, column, and 3×3 box needs 1–9 exactly once.

3 · Notes — pencil in candidates when you’re not sure yet.

4 · Hint — stuck? A hint fills one cell, but costs points.

5 · Erase & Undo — clear a cell, or take back your last move.
