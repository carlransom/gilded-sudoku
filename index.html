Point one · The look

Porcelain, not a spreadsheet.

Open any other sudoku app and you get a spreadsheet with numbers in it. Gilded Sudoku is built around the board itself: white porcelain tiles, deep black lines, and gold that lands when a number does. It feels less like data entry and more like setting a table you're proud of.

Three colors. That's the point.

White, black, and gold — nothing else competing for your eyes. The board stays calm, and the gold is saved for the moments that matter: a correct number, a finished row, a rank earned.

Smooth and shiny, on purpose

Every tile has the sheen of glazed ceramic, with soft sound effects as numbers settle in (and a mute toggle when you want silence). It's a small thing — until you've played on a flat gray grid and come back.

Made for real hands

Big, comfortable number pad — with an even larger pad option in Settings — so a puzzle on the couch or in a waiting room never turns into a fight with tiny buttons.
