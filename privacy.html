Privacy Policy — Gilded Sudoku

Effective November 18, 2026 · Operator: Carl Ransom · Contact: carl.ransom@gmail.com

Gilded Sudoku is a free Sudoku game. You can play as a guest without signing in. If you sign in, your game progress can follow you across devices. This policy explains what is collected and why.

What we collect

  • If you play as a guest: your progress (score, rank, wins, badges, best times, daily streak, weekly goal, and your in-progress puzzle) is stored only on your own device, in your browser. It is never sent to us.
  • If you sign in with Google or email: we store your name and email address, and your game progress, so it can sync across your devices. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; your progress is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. We do not see or store your password.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

Advertising

Gilded Sudoku is free and supported by ads. We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

You can remove ads entirely with a one-time in-app purchase ("Remove Ads", $2.99) once that option is available in the store version of the game.

How we use your information

Only to run the game: to save and sync your progress, to show you your own scores and ranks, and to keep the service working. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers above (Google Firebase for sign-in and storage, Google AdSense for ads), who process it only to provide those services.

Children

Gilded Sudoku is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • You can play without signing in at any time.
  • You can sign out in the game; your on-device progress stays on your device.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and your synced progress by emailing carl.ransom@gmail.com.
  • You can clear your browser's site data to erase on-device progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: carl.ransom@gmail.com. Gilded Sudoku is operated by Carl Ransom, Florida, USA.
